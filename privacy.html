Desi Spy - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Overview

Desi Spy is a pass-and-play party game. It has no accounts, no ads and no in-app purchases. The app collects anonymous usage data so we can understand how the game is played and improve it. It does not collect your name, the names of players, or anything you type into the app.

Earlier versions of Desi Spy (1.0.3 and below) collected no data at all. This policy applies to versions released after November 18, 2026.

Information We Collect

The app sends the following to our analytics provider:

  • A random identifier. Created by the app the first time it runs. It is not your name, Apple ID or advertising identifier, and it is replaced if you delete and reinstall the app.
  • App activity. When the app is opened and closed, how long it stays open, and which screens are viewed.
  • Game settings and results. The number of players and spies, the categories chosen, whether player names, a custom category or the timer were used, how many rounds were played and how long they lasted.
  • Device details. Operating system and version, app version, and the device's language setting.
  • Approximate location. City, region and country, estimated from your IP address. The app never asks for or uses your device's precise location.

Information We Don't Collect

  • Player names you enter
  • Custom category names, or the words you add to them
  • The secret word in any round
  • Your name, email address, phone number or Apple ID
  • Precise location, photos or contacts
  • Advertising identifiers

How We Use This Information

We use it only to see how Desi Spy is used, such as how often it is opened, which categories are popular and how many people usually play, so we can improve the game. We do not sell this information, use it for advertising, or use it to track you across other apps or websites.

Third-Party Services

Usage data is processed and stored on our behalf by Mixpanel, an analytics company based in the United States. Mixpanel receives your IP address when the app sends data and uses it to estimate your approximate location. The app does not use any advertising networks.

Information Stored on Your Device

Custom categories you create are saved only on your device and are never uploaded. They stay there until you delete the category or the app. Player names are held in memory while the app is running and are not saved or uploaded.

Playing Offline

Desi Spy works without an internet connection. Usage data recorded while your device is offline is sent the next time the app is used with a connection.

Your Choices

The data is not linked to your name or contact details, so we cannot tell which records belong to you. Deleting the app removes the random identifier and everything the app stored on your device. If you have questions or concerns about your data, contact us using the details below.

Children's Privacy

Desi Spy is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we make any changes to this privacy policy in future updates, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
